Matel: Building the Machines Inside India's Electric Vehicles

What Is Matel?

Matel Motion & Energy Solutions is a Pune-based deeptech company building electric motors and drivetrain systems — the core mechanical and electrical components that power electric vehicles, rather than the vehicles themselves. This positions Matel as a supplier to India's broader EV manufacturing ecosystem, benefiting from the same underlying demand growth as EV vehicle makers, but with a different, more B2B-oriented business model.

Business Model

Matel operates as a component and systems supplier to EV manufacturers, generating revenue by selling motors and drivetrain technology to vehicle makers rather than selling directly to consumers. This "picks and shovels" positioning — supplying critical components to the broader EV industry rather than competing directly for consumer vehicle sales — can offer a differentiated risk profile compared to consumer-facing EV brands, since Matel's success depends on the overall growth of India's EV manufacturing sector broadly, not on winning market share in any single vehicle category.

Why EV Drivetrain Technology Matters

As India's EV manufacturing sector has grown, the availability of high-quality, locally-manufactured drivetrain components has become a strategic bottleneck — many EV makers, particularly newer entrants, have historically relied on imported components, adding cost and supply chain risk. Companies like Matel building indigenous drivetrain technology are positioned to benefit from both rising EV demand and policy pushes toward domestic manufacturing and component localization in India's automotive sector.

Market Position

Matel operates in the EV components and deeptech manufacturing space, a category that has attracted growing investor interest as India's broader EV ecosystem matures beyond just vehicle assembly toward deeper domestic manufacturing capability — including batteries, motors, and power electronics. This "vehicle-agnostic" B2B supplier positioning means Matel's growth is tied to the overall expansion of India's EV industry rather than any single vehicle brand's success.

What the Funding Will Likely Support

Series B capital for a drivetrain/motor manufacturing company typically supports manufacturing capacity expansion, R&D for next-generation motor efficiency and performance, and building out relationships with a broader base of EV manufacturer customers.
