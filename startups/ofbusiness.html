OfBusiness: India's B2B Ecommerce Unicorn Eyes an $800 Million IPO

B2B E-commerce · Fintech · Updated August 2026 · 7 min read

OfBusiness, one of India's most prominent B2B ecommerce unicorns, is reportedly targeting a Draft Red Herring Prospectus (DRHP) filing by November 2026, for an IPO of up to $800 million, split between a fresh issue and an offer for sale.

What Is OfBusiness?

OfBusiness is a B2B ecommerce platform focused on procurement for small and medium enterprises (SMEs), primarily in raw materials and industrial goods — connecting manufacturers and businesses with suppliers while also offering embedded financing to help SMEs manage working capital for large procurement orders. This combination of ecommerce and fintech is central to the company's business model and a key part of what pushed it to unicorn valuation status.

Business Model

OfBusiness operates on a two-pronged model:

  1. B2B procurement marketplace — connecting businesses (particularly SMEs and manufacturers) with suppliers of raw materials and industrial goods, earning margin or transaction fees on procurement volume
  2. Embedded SME financing — offering working capital and supply chain financing to business customers, addressing a major pain point for SMEs that often struggle to access traditional bank credit for large procurement needs

This dual model — commerce plus embedded finance — has been a differentiator versus purely transactional B2B marketplaces, since the financing layer both drives platform stickiness and generates additional revenue.

IPO Plans

OfBusiness is reportedly reviving its public listing plans, targeting a DRHP filing by November 2026 for an IPO sized at up to $800 million, structured as a combination of a fresh issue (new capital raised for the company) and an offer for sale (existing shareholders selling shares). This would make it one of the larger Indian startup IPOs in the current cycle, reflecting continued institutional confidence in the B2B ecommerce and embedded finance model despite broader market selectivity around startup listings.

Market Position & Competition

OfBusiness operates in India's B2B procurement and SME financing space, a sector with significant addressable market given India's large base of small and medium manufacturers, many of whom remain underserved by traditional banking and legacy procurement processes. Competitors in adjacent spaces include other B2B commerce and SME fintech players, though OfBusiness's combined commerce-plus-financing approach has been a distinctive part of its scale story.

Why This IPO Matters for India's Startup Ecosystem

A successful $800 million IPO from OfBusiness would be a significant data point for India's broader startup listing pipeline — particularly for B2B and fintech-adjacent companies, a category that has seen more selective public market reception than pure consumer internet plays in recent IPO cycles.

India's SME Financing Gap

Small and medium enterprises in India have historically struggled to access adequate working capital financing through traditional banking channels, often due to limited credit history, lack of collateral, or the sheer operational cost banks face in underwriting large numbers of small-ticket loans. This financing gap has created significant opportunity for platforms like OfBusiness that combine commerce data (which gives them visibility into a business's actual transaction volume and reliability) with financing products — effectively using procurement behavior as a proxy for creditworthiness in ways traditional banks often can't replicate.

Why B2B Ecommerce Plus Fintech Is a Powerful Combination

Pure B2B marketplaces often struggle with thin margins and platform stickiness, since business buyers can relatively easily switch suppliers if price is the only differentiator. By layering financing on top of commerce, companies like OfBusiness create a stickier relationship — a business that's using the platform's working capital financing has a much stronger reason to continue routing its procurement volume through the same platform, since switching would mean also finding alternative financing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does OfBusiness do?

OfBusiness is a B2B ecommerce platform connecting SMEs and manufacturers with suppliers of raw materials and industrial goods, combined with embedded financing services for procurement working capital.

How big is OfBusiness's planned IPO?

OfBusiness is reportedly targeting an IPO of up to $800 million, with a DRHP filing expected by November 2026.

Is OfBusiness a unicorn?

Yes, OfBusiness has previously achieved unicorn status (a valuation over $1 billion), reflecting its scale in India's B2B ecommerce and SME financing space.

What is a DRHP filing?

A Draft Red Herring Prospectus (DRHP) is a preliminary document companies file with securities regulators ahead of an IPO, disclosing financial and business details to prospective investors before the public listing process proceeds.
