Clean Energy · Rooftop Solar · Updated August 2026 · 7 min read

SolarSquare raised $53 million in a Series C round led by B Capital, with a notable investment from former Indian cricket captain MS Dhoni's family office, alongside participation from existing investors.

What Is SolarSquare?

SolarSquare operates in India's rooftop solar installation space, helping residential and commercial customers install solar panel systems — a category benefiting from both rising electricity costs (making solar increasingly cost-competitive) and government incentive schemes promoting rooftop solar adoption as part of India's renewable energy targets.

Business Model

Rooftop solar companies like SolarSquare typically generate revenue through system sales and installation services, sometimes supplemented by financing options that let customers pay for solar installations over time rather than upfront — a significant factor in residential solar adoption, since the upfront cost of a full system remains a meaningful barrier for many Indian households despite the long-term electricity savings.

Why a Celebrity Family Office Investment Matters

MS Dhoni's family office investment in SolarSquare is notable both for the direct capital and for the visibility it brings to the rooftop solar category among a broader Indian audience — celebrity-linked investments often signal growing mainstream confidence in a sector, beyond just specialized clean energy VCs, and can meaningfully boost consumer brand awareness for the company involved.

Why Rooftop Solar Is Attracting Capital in India

India's rooftop solar market has grown alongside rising grid electricity costs, improving solar panel economics, and government schemes like PM Surya Ghar Yojana aimed at accelerating residential solar adoption. This combination of favorable unit economics and policy tailwinds has made rooftop solar installation companies an increasingly attractive category for climate-focused and generalist investors alike.

Market Position & Competition

SolarSquare operates in a competitive but growing rooftop solar installation market, alongside other Indian solar installers and EPC (engineering, procurement, construction) companies serving both residential and commercial customers. Differentiation in this category typically comes from installation quality, financing options offered to customers, and after-sales service — factors that matter significantly given solar systems are long-term investments requiring years of reliable performance.

What the Funding Will Likely Support

Series C capital for a rooftop solar company typically supports geographic expansion into new markets, building out installation and service capacity, and potentially expanding financing options to make solar more accessible to a broader customer base.
