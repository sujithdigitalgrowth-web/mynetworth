Zetwerk: SEBI Clears the Path for a $450 Million IPO

B2B Manufacturing · IPO Watch · Updated August 2026 · 8 min read

Zetwerk, the B2B manufacturing unicorn, has received SEBI's approval for its initial public offering — the latest step in a winding IPO journey that saw the company delay, reroute, and finally reappoint bankers before filing confidentially in March 2026 for a targeted $450 million listing.

What Is Zetwerk?

Zetwerk is a B2B manufacturing platform founded in 2018 by Amrit Acharya, Srinath Ramakkrushnan, Vishal Chaudhary, Ankit Fatehpuria, and Rahul Sharma, connecting manufacturers with vendors and suppliers of industrial machine components. The company operates a technology-enabled manufacturing platform serving sectors including energy, electronics, defence, aerospace, and capital goods — essentially acting as an intermediary layer that helps businesses source manufacturing capacity and components more efficiently than through traditional, fragmented supplier relationships.

Business Model

Zetwerk generates revenue by connecting businesses needing manufactured components with a network of vendors and suppliers, taking a role in coordinating, quality-assuring, and often financing these transactions — a model that addresses genuine fragmentation and inefficiency in traditional industrial manufacturing sourcing, particularly for mid-sized manufacturers who lack the scale to build direct relationships with the best suppliers themselves.

Why Zetwerk's IPO Journey Is Worth Following

Zetwerk's back-and-forth IPO timeline — delaying to 2027, then reversing course and moving forward within the same year — reflects broader uncertainty many large Indian startups have faced around public market timing and receptiveness. The eventual scaling down from an initially discussed ~$500M/~$5B valuation target to a $450M IPO also suggests some recalibration of expectations, a pattern seen across several Indian startup IPOs navigating more selective public market conditions than the peak funding years.

Market Position & Competition

Zetwerk operates in India's B2B manufacturing and supply chain technology space, with its multi-sector reach (energy, electronics, defence, aerospace, capital goods) giving it a broader addressable market than more narrowly-focused manufacturing marketplaces. Its scale — having raised nearly $800 million to date — positions it among the most well-capitalized players in Indian B2B manufacturing technology.

What Public Market Investors Will Watch For

As highlighted by Orios Venture Partners' Rehan Yar Khan in coverage of the IPO, public market investors are expected to place greater emphasis on governance, capital efficiency, and long-term value creation — favouring companies balancing scale with financial prudence, a bar that reflects lessons learned from earlier Indian startup IPOs that faced post-listing scrutiny over growth-at-all-costs approaches.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Zetwerk do?

Zetwerk operates a B2B technology platform connecting manufacturers with vendors and suppliers of industrial machine components, serving sectors including energy, electronics, defence, aerospace, and capital goods.

How much has Zetwerk raised, and what's its IPO size?

Zetwerk has raised over $793 million to date from investors including Greenoaks Capital, Lightspeed Venture Partners, and Peak XV Partners, and is targeting a $450 million IPO after receiving SEBI approval.

Who founded Zetwerk?

Zetwerk was founded in 2018 by Amrit Acharya, Srinath Ramakkrushnan, Vishal Chaudhary, Ankit Fatehpuria, and Rahul Sharma.

Why did Zetwerk's IPO timeline change multiple times?

Zetwerk initially targeted a $500M IPO in February 2025, delayed to 2027 mid-year, then reversed course again by November 2025, ultimately filing confidentially in March 2026 for a $450M offering — reflecting broader market timing considerations common among large Indian startup IPOs.
