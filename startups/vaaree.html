What Is Vaaree?

Vaaree is a Bengaluru-based home décor and furnishings marketplace, founded in 2022 by Garima and Varun Vohra along with their founding team. The platform offers over 1.5 lakh products spanning home décor and furnishing categories, positioning it as a broad-catalog online destination for Indian consumers looking to furnish and decorate their homes without the fragmentation of shopping across multiple specialty retailers.

Business Model

Vaaree operates as an online marketplace/retailer for home décor and furnishings, generating revenue through product sales across a wide catalog. This category — home décor and furnishings — has traditionally been underserved by large-scale organized e-commerce in India relative to categories like fashion or electronics, with much of the market still served by local retailers, giving platforms like Vaaree room to capture demand from consumers seeking wider selection and organized online shopping for home goods.

Market Position & Competition

Vaaree competes in India's growing online home décor and furnishings space alongside players like Pepperfry and Urban Ladder in furniture-adjacent categories, though its specific focus on décor (rather than heavier furniture logistics) may allow for a different, potentially more asset-light operating model — décor products are generally easier and cheaper to ship and store than large furniture items, which changes the unit economics meaningfully.

Why Home Décor E-commerce Is an Attractive Category

India's home décor and furnishings market has grown alongside rising urban homeownership, renovation activity, and a broader consumer shift toward personalizing living spaces — trends accelerated in part by increased time spent at home in recent years globally. Unlike furniture, which faces real logistics complexity (large item shipping, assembly, returns), décor products are generally lighter, easier to ship, and support higher purchase frequency, which can make for a more capital-efficient e-commerce model at scale.

What the Funding Will Likely Support

Series A capital for a marketplace at this stage typically supports catalog expansion, improving supply chain and fulfillment infrastructure, and marketing spend to build brand awareness in a category where consumer trust and taste-matching (a major factor in décor purchasing decisions) both matter significantly.
