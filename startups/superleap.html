What Is Superleap?

Superleap is a Bengaluru-based enterprise AI startup building an AI-native customer relationship management (CRM) platform for large sales and revenue teams. Founded by Subham Kumar Boundia, Alok Maurya, and Saurabh Maheshwari — all former senior operators at Unacademy — the company positions itself as an "agentic operating system" where AI agents and human sales teams work together across the entire revenue function, rather than a traditional CRM that simply logs data.

Business Model

Superleap sells enterprise software on a contract basis to large organizations, with reported average contract values between $60,000 and $100,000 — reflecting its focus on large enterprise customers rather than small and medium businesses. The platform has three core components: SuperOS (data management), SuperSense (customer intelligence), and SuperAgents (task automation across the sales process), alongside AI voice bots and integrations with WhatsApp, Slack, Microsoft Teams, and major AI assistants.

Real Traction Behind the Round

Unlike many early-stage enterprise software companies, Superleap's funding announcement came with concrete usage data: Razorpay transferred over 10 million records to the platform in 45 days, and Aakash Education (a major Indian test-prep company) moved approximately 5,000 counsellors across 400 locations onto the platform in under 60 days. The company also reports serving customers across financial services, software, education, healthcare, travel, hospitality, and media — a genuinely broad enterprise customer base for a company at this funding stage.

Why AI-Native CRM Is a Compelling Category

Traditional CRM platforms have existed for decades, but most were built before large language models made natural-language automation and AI agents practical. Superleap's pitch — replacing legacy database-centric CRM workflows with AI agents that can act autonomously across the sales process — reflects a broader wave of "AI-native" rebuilds of established enterprise software categories, where incumbents face genuine disruption risk from newer platforms built AI-first rather than retrofitted with AI features.

Market Position

Superleap targets large enterprise sales and revenue teams specifically, rather than initially pursuing the more crowded small-business CRM market — a strategic choice that reduces competition from established players who typically dominate the SMB segment, while requiring the company to prove enterprise-grade reliability and integration capability from an early stage.

What the Funding Will Support

Per the company, funds will support core machine learning model optimization, expanding its pipeline of autonomous task agents, and accelerating enterprise customer acquisition across global mid-market financial and commercial sectors, alongside international expansion and hiring.
