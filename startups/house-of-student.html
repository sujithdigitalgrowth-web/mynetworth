What Is House of Student?

House of Student is a Gurugram and US-based student living technology company operating a platform for student accommodation across global education destinations, offering online booking and housing network management. This positions the company at the intersection of India's large outbound international student population and the broader global student housing market — a category that has historically been fragmented and difficult to navigate for students moving abroad for education.

Business Model

House of Student likely generates revenue through booking commissions on student accommodation reservations, and potentially through partnerships with property owners/managers who list housing inventory on its platform — a marketplace model connecting international students (particularly those from India, given the company's Gurugram base) with vetted housing options in destination countries, addressing a genuine pain point since finding trustworthy accommodation from abroad is a well-documented source of stress and risk for international students.

Why Student Housing Is an Underserved Global Market

India sends a significant and growing number of students abroad each year for higher education, and the process of securing safe, verified accommodation from a different country — often without being able to physically visit in advance — remains a genuine pain point. Platforms addressing this gap combine real-estate marketplace dynamics with trust and verification needs specific to a customer base (students, often moving countries for the first time) that has particular vulnerability to housing scams and unreliable listings.

Market Position

House of Student's dual Gurugram-US presence suggests a strategy of serving Indian students heading to US and other international destinations specifically, rather than a purely domestic Indian student housing play — a more focused go-to-market than broader proptech platforms that might address domestic housing needs generally.

What the Funding Will Likely Support

Seed capital combining equity and debt typically supports platform development, building out verified housing inventory in key destination markets, and initial marketing to reach students planning international education moves — the debt component may specifically support working capital needs tied to housing transactions or guarantees.
